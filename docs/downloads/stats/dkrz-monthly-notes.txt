DKRZ monthly usage statistics

File: dkrz-monthly.csv

Format: UTF-8, semicolon delimiter, decimal point, six columns:
date;country;requests;downloads;downloads_size;visits

Each row represents a calendar month. The date is the month-start label, not
the date of an individual request. DKRZ only; IPSL and CEDA are excluded.
The file is refreshed by scripts/aggregate_dashboard_year.py --all-years.
Its first and last rows identify the current coverage. No extrapolation is applied.
2021 request reporting starts in March.

Country GB is the agreed attribution for CDS gateway traffic, matching the
previous UK convention. It is not the country of each underlying CDS user.
Visits are empty because distinct users cannot be determined from these
aggregated dashboards. Requests, downloads, and concurrency are not visits.

Requests are dkrz_requests in roocs-<year>-monthly.csv, matching
the annual reports. They include successful and failed processing requests.
Downloads are the sum of the DKRZ "Downloads per day" request_type series,
filtered to each reporting month. These are recorded fetch counts, not
distinct output filenames. Download counts need not equal request counts.
downloads_size uses dkrz_subsetted_data_gb in those same annual-report CSVs,
converted with 1 GB = 1024 MB, consistent with the previous project exports.
Source size totals are rounded, so the exported sizes are approximate.

Known source limitations:
- 2021: DKRZ transfer totals only cover July-December. The annual DKRZ
  download series has no observations for March-June; monthly DKRZ download
  exports are available for July-December. Downloads and downloads_size are
  therefore empty for March-June, rather than reporting unknown values as zero.
- May 2024: Downloads combine May 1-27 from the partial DKRZ export and
  May 30-31 from the regular export. These dates do not overlap. May 28-29
  are missing, so the May 2024 download count is a recorded lower bound.
  Size follows the annual report's 3,328.50 GB May estimate from the partial
  export; the regular export contains only the final 5.60 GB.
- 2026: Monthly exports are used throughout, as in the annual report.
  Quarterly exports differ from the sum of the monthly exports.
